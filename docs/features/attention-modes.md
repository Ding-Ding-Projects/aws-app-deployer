# Attention modes

Five separate modes in **Settings > Focus and comfort**. Each one is off until you turn it on, and any mix works. They are interface options, not medical advice.

- **Focus:** dims everything except the row, card or setting you are on. Nothing is hidden; hover or move keyboard focus to bring anything forward.
- **Low stimulation:** quieter colour, no extra motion, and only notifications that need you pop up. Every message still lands in the notification list. Your system's reduced-motion setting is always respected as well.
- **Time awareness:** the title bar shows how long the app has been open and how long since you last changed something. Just the numbers.
- **One thing at a time:** write one next action; it stays at the top of every screen and survives a restart. **Done** clears it.
- **Momentum:** when a deployment draft has not changed for 30 minutes, a small note offers to reopen it. **Not now** hides it for one hour.

The landing page has the same five modes in its accessibility tools, adapted to a page: Focus dims other sections, Time awareness shows time on the page, and Momentum appears after 10 quiet minutes. School mode leaves all five available.

## Funny levels

English and Cantonese each have their own funny level from 1 (plain) to 5 (very playful). Both start at 5. The level styles every kind of message, errors and warnings included, while names, accounts, numbers and errors stay exact. School mode uses level 1.
