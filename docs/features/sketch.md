# Sketch

Open **New tab > Sketch** to draw, or press **Image** to draw on a PNG, JPEG or WebP picture.

- **Pens:** Pencil (1), Pen (2), Fineliner (3), Marker (4), Highlighter (5), Brush (6), Fountain pen (7) and an Eraser (E) that removes only what it passes over, including part of a stroke. Pencil, Pen and Brush get thinner as you draw faster; Fineliner and Highlighter keep one width; the Fountain pen changes with direction. Hold Shift to lock a stroke to one of eight directions.
- **Toolbar:** colour, size, undo (Ctrl+Z), redo (Ctrl+Y), clear (super confirmation), and a minimise button that collapses it to the active tool.
- **Export:** SVG of exactly what is shown, erasing included, and PNG at 1x, 2x or 3x.
- Strokes are saved on this computer, appear in local history, and come back after a restart.
